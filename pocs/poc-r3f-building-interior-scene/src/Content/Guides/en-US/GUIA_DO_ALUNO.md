## How to walk

| Key | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| **W** or **↑** | walk forward |
| **S** or **↓** | walk back |
| **A** or **←** | turn left |
| **D** or **→** | turn right |
| **C** | toggle between the avatar and the orbital view |

## How to interact

Walk up to a building: the prompt **"Press [E] to Enter or [I] for Information"** appears.

- **I** opens the building's information.
- **E** enters the room and shows its missions.
- **H** opens this guide, from anywhere.
- **Esc** closes the open window.

> While you type in a text field, shortcuts are turned off.

## Orbital view

Drag to rotate the camera and use the mouse wheel (or pinch) to zoom. On touch screens the campus opens in this view.

## On a phone (touch)

The campus opens in the **orbital view**. Tap **Avatar** to walk:

| Joystick gesture (bottom-left corner) | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| drag **up** | walk forward |
| drag **down** | walk back |
| drag **sideways** | turn to the same side |
| **let go** | the avatar stops |

Near a building, tap **Enter** or **Information** on the prompt. With a window open, the joystick hides and the avatar stays still.
