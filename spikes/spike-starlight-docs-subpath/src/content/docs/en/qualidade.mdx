---
title: "Quality and security"
description: "Tests, delivery gates and care with data."
---

- **Automated tests** in every layer: the domain with no screen, use cases with fake Ports, adapters with contract tests.
- **Gates on every pull request:** lint with the layer boundaries, type checking, tests and build. Without them, nothing is published.
- **No stale version in the browser:** pages without cache and files with a hash in their name.
- **Data:** credentials outside the public folder, nothing sensitive in the pages, and this documentation goes through a guard that prevents publishing server paths or environment files.
